# Internship Review & Reflection

Throughout my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd.**, spanning from **May 4, 2025, to July 25, 2025**, I had the privilege of participating in the **Workforce Bootcamp – First Cloud AI Journey** program. This invaluable experience allowed me to bridge the academic knowledge acquired at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HUTECH) with industrial practices by constructing and deploying a production-ready system on top of the AWS ecosystem.

During the execution phase, I actively contributed to developing the frontend user interface, architecting cloud system topologies on AWS, establishing secure VPC private networking configurations along with target data tiers, modeling relational databases with complex ERD schemas, conducting full-cycle functional website testing, and compiling comprehensive technical workshop documentation for system deployment. These core activities significantly enhanced my competencies in web development, cloud computing architecture, database management, systems analysis, software QA testing, technical writing, and professional reporting.

Regarding my workplace conduct and ethics, I consistently strived to deliver assigned tasks on schedule, strictly adhered to organizational guidelines, and proactively conducted research into emerging technologies. Furthermore, I maintained active communications with my faculty advisor and fellow team members to collaboratively troubleshoot technical bottlenecks, refine the product deliverables, and drive work efficiency.
