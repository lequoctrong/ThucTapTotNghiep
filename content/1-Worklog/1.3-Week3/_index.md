### Week 3 Objectives:

* Study centralized monitoring systems and resource management frameworks in cloud environments.
* Master the skills required to track system performance, analyze log infrastructure, and build automated alerting mechanisms.
* Familiarize with standard cost management dashboards and structural health assessment services based on AWS best practices.

### Week 3 Achievements:

* **Monitoring & Logging Operations Mindset:**
  * Mastered the essential principles of Amazon CloudWatch, clearly distinguishing between structured Metrics data, Aggregated Statistics, and system Logs on AWS.
  * Developed the capability to comfortably inspect historical logging trails and analyze the real-time operational efficiency of remote EC2 instances.
* **Automated Alerting Implementation:**
  * Successfully provisioned fully functional CloudWatch Alarms, establishing automated monitoring rules to capture critical resource spikes (e.g., high CPU thresholds).
* **Cost Governance & System Optimization:**
  * Gained comprehensive familiarity with the AWS Billing Dashboard to track, transparently manage, and evaluate operational cloud expenses.
  * Successfully initialized proactive budget configurations to keep track of operational thresholds, preventing unintended billing alerts during the internship period.
  * Grasped how to leverage continuous best-practice telemetry from AWS Trusted Advisor to safeguard system security, performance efficiency, and cost optimizations.
