>Here, you can freely contribute your personal feedback regarding your experience in the First Cloud AI Journey program, helping the FCAJ team improve on remaining shortcomings based on the following categories:

### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is very friendly and open. FCAJ members are always ready to assist whenever I face difficulties, even outside of working hours. The workspace is tidy and comfortable, which helps me focus better. However, I think adding some social gatherings or team-bonding activities could help everyone understand each other more.

**2. Support from Mentors / Team Admins**  
Mentors provide highly detailed guidance, explain clearly when I do not understand, and constantly encourage me to ask questions. The admin team effectively assists with procedures and documentation, creating favorable conditions for my work. I highly appreciate that mentors allow me to experiment and troubleshoot problems on my own instead of simply giving out the answers.

**3. Alignment between Work and Major**  
The assigned tasks align well with the knowledge I acquired at university, while also expanding into new domains that I had never accessed before. As a result, I am able to both consolidate my foundational knowledge and acquire practical field skills.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
During the internship, I learned many new skills, such as using project management tools, teamwork skills, and professional workplace communication. Mentors also shared a wealth of practical experiences that helped guide my career path better.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is highly positive: everyone respects one another, working seriously yet maintaining a cheerful atmosphere. When faced with urgent deadlines, everyone puts in common efforts and supports each other regardless of role or position. This makes me feel like an integral part of the collective, even as an intern.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
The company provides an internship allowance and offers flexible scheduling when necessary. In addition, being granted access to internal training sessions is a major plus point.


### Other Questions

* **What are you most satisfied with during your internship?**
  * It is definitely the well-structured roadmap of the 12-week program. I had hands-on exposure ranging from core Cloud services (EC2, S3) to advanced architectures (Serverless, Docker, CI/CD). The close combination of theoretical learning and Console/CLI practice boosted my practical technical capabilities rapidly.
* **What do you think the company needs to improve for future interns?**
  * During the initial weeks on VPC (Networking) or CI/CD, the workload and knowledge volume can be quite heavy for students. The organizing team could provide a glossary or pre-read materials about a week in advance so that interns do not feel overwhelmed.
* **If recommending to friends, would you advise them to intern here? Why?**
  * Absolutely yes. This is one of the rare environments that allows interns to directly operate and build infrastructure on real Cloud environments, backed by extremely close guidance from experienced Mentors.


### Proposals & Desires

* **What proposals do you have to improve the internship experience?**
  * I propose that the organizing team design small group assignments (Mini-hackathons) among interns mid-term to enhance bonding and cross-learning.
* **Would you like to continue with this program in the future?**
  * I highly desire the opportunity to continue accompanying the program, or to participate in deeper projects within the company as an official Cloud Engineer.
* **Other feedback (free to share):**
  * I would like to express my sincere gratitude to all the Mentors and Admins of the FCAJ program for their dedicated guidance, support, and for creating the best possible conditions for me throughout the past 3 months.
